Hotel

Where you’ll be staying.

Aki Hotel Hong Kong

Hong Kong · 3–11 December

239 Jaffe Road, Wan Chai, Hong Kong Island, Hong Kong

A calm, design-led base in the heart of Wan Chai. Its 173 contemporary rooms have light natural wood, high ceilings and large windows, and include what were Hong Kong’s first Japanese-style tatami rooms. There’s a rooftop whisky bar with panoramic city views.

Wan Chai MTR station is a five-minute walk, the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre eight minutes, and Times Square about six. Causeway Bay’s shopping district is close by.

Important notes

Read before you pack.

Travel insurance is strongly recommended.

Equipment. Bring whatever camera you want. Your host travels light and usually shoots one camera. You’ll be on and off trains and in and out of taxis, so use a small bag if you’re bringing multiple lenses.
